A viewer for large 4D scientific image data.

It opens OME-TIFF and OME-Zarr, and other formats through plugins.

Download for Linux, Windows & macOS

vizi 1.0.0 — Linux, Windows, and macOS on Apple Silicon.

  • Opens OME-TIFF and OME-Zarr
  • Scrubs and plays files over 10 GB at full resolution
  • 2D slices and a 3D view that stay in step
  • Regions you can draw, measure over time and crop to
  • Per-channel colors and contrast
  • Export of images, movies and data
  • Plugins that add file formats and tools
