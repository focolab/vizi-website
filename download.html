Get vizi for your platform.

vizi runs natively on macOS, Windows, and Linux — Vulkan, Metal, and DX12 GPU backends through wgpu. It opens OME-TIFF, OME-Zarr, and FOCO Lab SCAPE natively, plus CZI, ND2, LIF and ~150 more formats via the optional Bio-Formats image server.
